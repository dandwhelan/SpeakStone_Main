# Changelog

## 2.0.7

**What's new**
- Lots more in-game gossip found and voiced, thanks to data shared by players.
- Lots more books found and voiced.
- A new book interface for reading and listening to in-game books.
- A new narrator voice.
- Quality-of-life improvements across the addon.

**Fixes**
- Fixed some characters using a voice of the wrong gender or race.

**Thank you**
A huge thanks to everyone who shared their game data this week: AuthenticSenpai, silvy, Pappnase, illset and Benny McBackstab, plus everyone who contributed anonymously. Thanks as well to Benny McBackstab and Pappnase for reporting voices that didn't match their NPCs. You can share your own data or report an issue at https://speakstone.beanw.co.uk.
